BEFORE YOU BOOK

Worth checking first, it might save you a call-out

  • Look at the pressure gaugeMost combis lock out below 1 bar. If yours has dropped, the filling loop in your manual will bring it back up to around 1 to 1.5 bar. If it keeps dropping, that's a fault worth booking.
  • Press reset onceIf it fires up and stays on, great. If it locks out again, leave it alone and book in. Resetting over and over can hide what's actually wrong.
  • In a cold snap, check the condensate pipeIt's the white or grey plastic pipe running outside from the boiler. If it's frozen, pour warm water over it (not boiling), then reset.
  • Prepayment meter?Check there's credit and that the hob still lights. If the hob's dead too, it's the gas supply rather than the boiler.
  • Timer and thermostatFlat batteries and a clock that's wrong after the hour changes catch more people out than you'd think.

Tried all that and it's still not working? Book a repair or send a photo of the boiler display on WhatsApp.

FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

Can you come out today?

Often, but I won't promise it until I've seen the diary. When you book you see the earliest free slot straight away. Some days that's the same day, on a busy day it's the next day.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

No. Repairs are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Booking is open 24/7 though, so you can get a slot in the diary at midnight on a Saturday and know exactly when I'm coming.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Don't book a repair. Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 straight away. It's free and open 24 hours. Open the windows, don't use light switches or naked flames, and leave the property if you can. Once they've made it safe, I can do the repair.

How much does an emergency boiler repair cost?

The same as any repair visit. There's no emergency surcharge. The visit is £100, which covers the diagnostic and the first hour, and plenty of faults are fixed inside that hour. If it needs a part or more time, I give you the price first and you decide. On my 2026 repairs the middle bill was about £170 all in, and most came in under £200.

Which boilers do you repair?

All the major brands. I carry common parts for Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal and Baxi on the van, so a lot of faults are fixed in one visit.

Is there anything I can try before booking?

Check the pressure gauge, press reset once, and in cold weather check the plastic condensate pipe outside hasn't frozen. If you're on a prepayment meter, check there's credit. If it's still not working after that, book in.
