FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

How much does it cost to fix a dripping tap?

£100 for the first hour, which covers most cartridge or washer replacements. Ceramic disc cartridges are the most common fix.

Can you fix a tap or do I need a new one?

Most modern taps are repairable. Mixer taps with ceramic cartridges, kitchen monobloc taps, traditional pillar taps. If the tap body is corroded a replacement makes more sense.

How long does a tap repair take?

Most repairs are under an hour, including isolating the supply and testing afterwards.

Why is my tap juddering or hammering?

Usually a worn cartridge or an issue with the supply pipework being unsecured. Both fixable.
