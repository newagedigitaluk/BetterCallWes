FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

How much does a new combi boiler cost installed?

A like-for-like combi swap in Southampton is typically £1,800 to £2,500 all-in. A relocation or system change pushes it higher.

Which combi boiler is best?

Worcester Bosch is the most reliable in my experience. Vaillant is the most efficient. Ideal is the best value. I recommend based on your house and budget.

How long do new combis last?

With annual servicing, 12 to 15 years. Most manufacturers offer 7 to 10 year warranties on new installs, longer if a certified installer fits it.

Can I get a combi installed in a flat?

Usually yes. Flue route and gas supply have to be right but most flats are fine. I'll survey before quoting.
