SHOWER TYPES

Which Shower Type Do You Have?

Electric Showers

Heat water on demand from the cold supply. Independent of your boiler – great for backup.

Brands: Mira, Triton, Aqualisa, Gainsborough

Mixer Showers

Blend hot and cold water from your existing supply. Relies on your boiler/hot water system.

Better flow than electric, needs good water pressure

Power Showers

Mixer shower with built-in pump. Ideal for homes with gravity-fed hot water and low pressure.

Not suitable for combi boilers

FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

What types of showers can you install?

Mixer showers, electric showers, power showers with pumps, and digital shower systems.

How much does a new shower installation cost?

£200 to £450 fitted depending on type. Electric showers at the lower end, pumped mains-fed setups at the top.

Do you tile and seal the shower area?

I focus on plumbing. I work alongside trusted local tilers if you need a full bathroom refit, or I'll fit into your existing tiled enclosure.

Can I have a power shower in a flat?

Sometimes. Mains-fed flats often have enough pressure without a pump. I test the supply pressure during the survey.
