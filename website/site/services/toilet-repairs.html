FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

My toilet won't stop running. Can you fix it?

Yes. It's almost always a fill or flush valve worn out. Usually a same-visit fix.

How much for a new toilet seat fitting?

Soft-close seats £40 to £70 supplied and fitted, including the trip out.

Can you unblock a toilet?

Yes. Plunger and basic auger usually clears it. If it needs jetting I quote that separately.

Is a wall-hung toilet harder to fit?

It is, yes. The cistern goes behind the wall in a frame, so you're paying for the framing plus the toilet. About £450 to £700 fitted depending on the unit.
