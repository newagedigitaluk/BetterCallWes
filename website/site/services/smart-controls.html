FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

Which smart heating controls do you fit?

Nest, Hive, Tado, Honeywell, Drayton Wiser. I'm happy to fit whichever you've picked.

Will a smart thermostat work with my old boiler?

Most modern smart controls work with most boilers made in the last 15 years. Older systems sometimes need a relay. I check during the survey.

Can you fit smart TRVs as well as the main thermostat?

Yes. Per-room control through smart TRVs is one of the biggest wins for energy use, especially in larger houses.

How long does a smart control install take?

Single thermostat 1 to 2 hours. Full multi-zone setup with smart TRVs can take half a day.
