Noisy Pipes

FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

How long does a full central heating install take?

A complete new system in an average house takes 3 to 5 days depending on radiator count and pipework.

Can you upgrade my radiators?

Yes. Single panels to doubles, towel rails, designer rads. I calculate the heat output the room needs rather than just matching the old one.

Should I get a power flush before new radiators?

If your existing system has any sludge, yes. Putting clean new rads on dirty pipework shortens their life. I check the system first.

Do you fit zone controls and smart thermostats?

Yes: Nest, Hive, Tado, Honeywell. Wired and wireless setups, including weather compensation if your boiler supports it.
