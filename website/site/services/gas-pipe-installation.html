FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

Can you run a new gas supply for a hob, fire, or extension?

Yes. I install gas pipework in copper or steel, sized for the appliance and tested to current standards.

Do I need building regs for a new gas pipe?

Notification under Gas Safe regulations is automatic when I do the work. You don't need to organise anything separately.

How much does new gas pipework cost?

Short runs (under 5m) typically £150 to £300. Longer runs through walls and floors depend on access and the route.

Can you connect an outdoor BBQ or patio heater?

Yes. External gas points need an isolation valve and weather-rated fittings. I can run a buried or surface-mounted line.
