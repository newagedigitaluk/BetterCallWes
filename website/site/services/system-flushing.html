FAQS

Common questions, plain answers

What's the difference between a power flush and a chemical flush?

Power flushing uses a pump to physically scour the system at low pressure. Chemical-only is cheaper but slower and won't shift heavy sludge.

How much does a system flush cost?

Chemical clean is £150 to £200. Full power flush £400 to £600 depending on radiator count.

How often should I flush my heating system?

Most systems benefit from a chemical clean every 5 to 7 years. Power flush only when needed (sludge, cold radiators, new boiler install).

Do new boiler installations need the old system flushed?

Yes. Manufacturer warranties usually require it. Installing a clean boiler on a dirty system invalidates the warranty.
