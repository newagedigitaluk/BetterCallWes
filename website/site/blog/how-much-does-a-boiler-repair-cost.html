How much does a boiler repair cost? My real 2026 prices

By Wes, Gas Safe Plumber & Heating Engineer Updated 19 October 2026 6 min read
Gas Safe Registered 5.0★ on Google, 200+ reviews in total 12-month workmanship guarantee SO14–SO53

The short answer: I went through every boiler repair I’ve invoiced this year. Across 27 repairs the middle bill was about £170, and that’s the whole bill, my £100 first hour plus the part. 16 of the 27 came in under £200. The dearest was £616, a Baxi that needed a fan, an expansion vessel and a safety valve.

Most boiler repair price guides online are national averages. These are my own numbers. I pulled every boiler repair I’ve invoiced since January 2026, when my rate went to £100 an hour, and left out services, installs and anything that wasn’t the boiler. What’s left is 27 repairs across Southampton and the towns around it.

How I charge for a boiler repair

The visit is £100. That covers finding the fault and the first hour of work. A lot of the time the fix fits inside that hour and the part is already on the van, so you pay the £100 plus the part and that’s the bill.

If it takes longer, extra time is £100 an hour. If I need to order a part, I tell you the price before I order it and you decide. Bigger parts sometimes mean a second visit. An emergency boiler repair costs the same as any other: there’s no surcharge for booking in a hurry.

Two things stand out. Leaks and pressure faults on Worcester and Vaillant boilers are the bread and butter, and they nearly all come in between £100 and £170. The bills over £300 are the moving parts (fans, pumps, gas valves), a circuit board, or a boiler with more than one thing wrong.

Another 12 visits this year ended at the £100 diagnosis. So the least a visit costs is £100, and on my jobs this year the most was £616.

Parts I haven’t fitted this year

One common repair didn’t come up in 2026: the diverter valve (hot water but no heating, or the other way round). My price list has it at £180 to £250.

The main heat exchanger is the one I’d stop and talk to you about. It’s the dearest part in the boiler, and on an older boiler the money is usually better spent on a new one. I’ve written an age-by-age repair or replace guide if you’re at that point.

“My boiler keeps losing pressure”

This is the call I get most. If you’re topping the pressure up every few days, it’s usually one of these:

  • A weeping pressure relief valve. A new one came to £120 to £150 this year.
  • A tired expansion vessel. Sometimes it only needs re-pressurising, which is labour, so it can fit in the first hour. A new one on a Vaillant was £315.
  • A leak on the boiler’s own pipework, like the supply manifold on a Worcester, at £149 to £157.

What it almost never is, is a reason to replace the boiler. If someone’s first answer to a pressure problem is “you need a new boiler”, get a second opinion.

How to avoid being overcharged

Worth knowing even if you never call me:

  1. Get the fault named first. “It’s the pressure relief valve” is a diagnosis you can check against the table above. “It’s playing up” is not.
  2. Ask for the part and the labour separately. Anyone straight with you won’t mind.
  3. Don’t replace a boiler for one cheap part. Most of the jobs in my table were under £200. That’s not a reason to spend £2,000 or more.

When I find the fault I tell you what it is, what the part costs and what the bill will be before I fit anything. If I think you’re better off replacing, I’ll say so and tell you why.

Repair now, or wait?

If the boiler still runs but something’s brewing (lockouts, a new noise, pressure creeping down), it’s easier to sort now than on the coldest night of the year, when everyone’s diary is full. The fix costs the same either way.

If it has stopped and you’ve no heating or hot water, book a visit rather than keep resetting it. Repeated lockouts can turn a cheap fix into a dear one.

FAQS

People also ask

How much does a boiler repair cost?

Across the 27 boiler repairs I invoiced in 2026, the middle bill was about £170, and that’s the whole bill: the £100 first hour plus the part. 16 of the 27 came in under £200. Bigger parts like a fan, pump, gas valve or circuit board took the bill to between £280 and £460, and the dearest was £616 for a boiler that needed three parts.

How much is your call-out fee?

£100. It covers the diagnosis and the first hour of work, so if the fix fits inside that hour you pay the £100 plus the part. Extra time is £100 an hour, and if a part has to be ordered I give you the price first.

Why does my boiler keep losing pressure, and what does it cost to fix?

Usually the pressure relief valve or the expansion vessel. A new relief valve came to £120 to £150 on my jobs this year. A new expansion vessel on a Vaillant was £315. Sometimes it’s a leak on the boiler’s own pipework, like the supply manifold on a Worcester, which was £149 to £157.

What is the most expensive boiler repair?

On my jobs this year, a gas valve at £454 and a circuit board at £450 were the dearest single parts, and £616 was the biggest bill, for a Baxi that needed a fan, an expansion vessel and a safety valve. A main heat exchanger costs more again, and on an older boiler that’s usually where I’d say put the money towards a new one.

Do you charge more for an emergency boiler repair?

No. An emergency repair is the same £100 visit as any other repair. You can book any time, online or on WhatsApp, and visits are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, usually the same day or the next.

Is it worth repairing a boiler or should I replace it?

The rule of thumb: if the repair is more than half the cost of a new boiler, or the boiler is over 12 to 15 years old and has needed two or more fixes recently, replacing is usually the better call. A single fault on a well-serviced boiler under about 10 years old is almost always worth repairing. There’s a full age-by-age guide on the blog.
