Boiler not producing hot water?

By Wes, Gas Safe Plumber & Heating Engineer 30 October 2026 6 min read

The short answer: If your combi boiler heats the radiators but not the hot water taps, the most likely cause is a failed diverter valve. If the boiler is doing nothing at all, look for a fault code on the display: it will usually point to low pressure, an ignition fault, a pump fault or a gas supply issue. Most hot-water-only faults in Southampton cost £100 to £250 to fix.

No hot water is one of the most urgent boiler calls, especially in autumn and winter. The good news is that the cause is usually specific, and for a combi boiler it is often one of only a handful of parts.

The most useful first question to ask yourself is this: does the boiler still heat the radiators? The answer splits the problem in two very different directions.

Radiators work, hot water taps don't: the diverter valve

A combi boiler heats one circuit at a time. When you turn on a tap, the diverter valve (sometimes called the three-port valve) redirects the boiler's heat from the central heating circuit to the domestic hot water plate exchanger. If the diverter valve sticks or wears out, the heat carries on going to the radiators and the taps run cold.

Signs that point to the diverter valve:

  • Radiators heat up normally but the hot water is cold, or only lukewarm.
  • If you turn the heating off at the thermostat and run the hot tap, the water gets hot. (The boiler fires for hot water when there is no heating demand.)
  • The boiler runs and the display shows no fault code.

A replacement diverter valve is in my price guide at £180 to £250 all in. I have not replaced one on a job yet in 2026 but it is a common repair I quote for. The diverter is usually a half-day job: order the part for the specific model and fit it on a return visit.

No heating and no hot water: start with the fault code

If the boiler is not producing heat at all, look at the display. Most modern boilers show an error code when they lock out. The code tells you (or your engineer) where to start looking. Common causes:

Low pressure lockout

Check the pressure gauge on the boiler front panel. If it reads below 0.5 bar, top it up using the filling loop until it reaches around 1.2 bar, then reset the boiler. If this fixes it, read the guide on why boilers keep losing pressure, because it will happen again without finding the source.

Ignition fault

If the boiler tries to fire but cannot light, you may hear a clicking or see an ignition error code. On my 2026 jobs an ignition fault on an Ideal (fault code L2) came to £153 for a new ignitor. A one-off reset can clear a temporary ignition lockout caused by a gas supply blip, but a fault that keeps returning needs diagnosing.

Pump fault

The pump circulates water around the system. A pump that has seized or failed means the boiler fires but heat cannot move around the system, and the boiler shuts itself off on a temperature overheat. On my 2026 jobs a pump replacement on a Vaillant came to £418 across two visits (the pump was ordered on the first visit).

Gas valve fault

A failed gas valve means the boiler cannot open the gas supply to fire at all. On my 2026 jobs a gas valve on a Glow-worm (fault code F28) came to £454. This is the dearer end of boiler repairs. On a boiler over 12 to 15 years old, this is one of the faults worth weighing against the cost of a new boiler. There is an age-by-age repair or replace guide if you are at that point.

Condensate pipe blockage (winter only)

Condensing boilers drain acidic water through a plastic pipe, often to an outside drain. In cold weather the condensate can freeze in the external section of the pipe, blocking it and causing the boiler to lock out. Pouring warm (not boiling) water over the frozen section usually clears it. This is one of the few faults that is safe and reasonable to try yourself before calling an engineer.

What about a system boiler or a boiler with a hot water cylinder?

System boilers and heat-only boilers store hot water in a cylinder. If the hot water is cold but the heating works, the fault is more likely to be in the cylinder circuit rather than the boiler itself: a motorised valve, a cylinder thermostat or the mid-position valve are the usual culprits. If the hot water is lukewarm but the cylinder feels warm, the immersion heater may have failed.

These faults are still diagnosed and quoted at the standard £100 first visit.

Should I reset the boiler?

One reset is fine. If the boiler restarts and the hot water comes back, watch it over the next day. If it locks out again there is a repeating fault that needs diagnosing. Resetting more than two or three times without finding the cause is not a good idea: on some faults it is not safe to keep restarting, and on others it can turn a cheap repair into a more expensive one.

FAQS

People also ask

Why does my boiler heat the radiators but not the hot water?

On a combi boiler, this is almost always a failed diverter valve. The diverter valve is what switches the boiler's heat between the central heating circuit and the domestic hot water. When it sticks or wears out, it sends heat to the radiators but not to the taps. A replacement diverter valve in Southampton costs £180 to £250 all in.

Why does my boiler have no hot water at all?

If the boiler is not producing heat for either the radiators or the taps, look for a fault code on the display. Common causes include a lockout (reset and monitor), low pressure (top up and check), a failed ignition, a faulty pump or a gas supply issue. On my 2026 Southampton jobs: an ignition fault (Ideal L2) cost £153, a pump replacement was £418 and a gas valve fault (Glow-worm F28) was £454.

Should I reset my boiler if there is no hot water?

One reset is fine. If the boiler restarts and the hot water comes back, watch it over the next day. If it locks out again, there is a repeating fault that needs diagnosing. Repeatedly resetting without finding the cause can turn a cheap fix into a more expensive one, and on some faults it is not safe to keep restarting the boiler.

How much does it cost to fix a boiler with no hot water in Southampton?

It depends on the cause. A diverter valve repair is £180 to £250. A pump is £280 to £420. A gas valve is typically around £454. A circuit board is £400 to £500. The diagnostic visit is £100 and covers finding the fault. If the fix is straightforward it is often done the same visit within that first hour.

Does a combi boiler have a hot water cylinder?

No. A combi (combination) boiler heats water on demand directly from the mains. There is no stored hot water tank. When you open the hot tap, the boiler fires to heat the water as it flows through. This is why a fault in the boiler is the first place to look when a combi gives no hot water.
