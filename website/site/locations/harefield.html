Common Questions from Harefield Residents

Do you cover the Thornhill and Harefield area for emergency plumbing?

Yes, Harefield, Thornhill and the surrounding east Southampton area is within my regular coverage zone. For urgent heating or plumbing issues I aim to attend the same day wherever possible.

My boiler is making a banging noise in Harefield, is that serious?

Banging or kettling noises from a boiler are usually caused by limescale build-up on the heat exchanger, which is common in areas with harder water. It's not an immediate emergency but it does reduce efficiency and can eventually cause the heat exchanger to fail. A chemical descale or system inhibitor treatment often resolves it.

How long does a boiler repair usually take?

Most boiler repairs are completed within two to three hours, depending on what the fault is. I carry a wide range of common spare parts in the van, which means many repairs can be completed in a single visit without waiting for parts to arrive.
