Victorian Terraces Need a Specialist Eye

Freemantle is dominated by late Victorian and Edwardian terraced housing built in the decades when Southampton was expanding rapidly toward the west. These properties are well built but their plumbing infrastructure is old, often original copper or iron pipework, gravity-fed tanks in the loft, and boilers that have been retrofitted into spaces not designed for them.

Lead pipework is still present in some Freemantle properties, particularly on the supply side from the street boundary. I carry out lead pipe replacement regularly in this part of Southampton. It's not always an emergency, but it's worth knowing about if your home pre-dates the 1970s and the pipework hasn't been touched.

FAQS

Common Questions from Freemantle Residents

Do you work on Victorian terraced houses in Freemantle?

Yes, Victorian terraces are some of the most common properties I work on across west Southampton. I'm very familiar with the plumbing and heating layouts typical of these homes, including gravity-fed systems, loft tanks, and the modifications that have been made to them over the decades.

I think I have lead pipes in my Freemantle home, what should I do?

If your home was built before the 1970s and you haven't had the pipework checked, it's worth arranging an inspection. Lead pipes are most commonly found on the supply pipe from the street into the house. I can inspect and, where necessary, replace lead pipework with modern copper or plastic, advising on the most cost-effective approach for your specific property.

Can you install a new boiler to replace a back boiler in Freemantle?

Yes, back boiler replacement is a job I carry out regularly in older west Southampton properties. Removing a back boiler and fitting a modern wall-mounted combi typically improves efficiency significantly and frees up space. I'll assess whether your existing pipework and radiators are suitable and give you a clear quote for the full installation.
