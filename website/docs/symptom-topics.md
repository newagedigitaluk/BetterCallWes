## Notes for Wes

- Rows 1 and 2 are ready to review in `website/site/blog/`. They are not linked anywhere
  and are not in the sitemap. Publishing is a separate step once Wes approves the topics.
- Volumes are national UK estimates. Southampton's local slice is small, but symptom
  searchers do not add a location, so a nationally-ranking article captures them.
- Rows 3 and 9 (boiler not working, central heating not working) have the biggest raw
  volume but are very broad. A page that names the fault codes and specific symptoms will
  rank better than a generic "boiler not working" page.
- Fault code pages (rows 6, 7, 11) are the clearest buying signal: someone with an F28
  on a Glow-worm already knows their boiler is broken. Each brand could be one page or
  one page per code family.
- The two drafts cover the highest-value, highest-confidence topics first. Approval of
  these two also serves as a check that the format and tone are right before more are
  written.
