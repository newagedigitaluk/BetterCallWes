## Losers and the cannibalisation behind them
Average position fell on the core boiler terms: "boiler repairs southampton" 15.8 to 26.5, "boiler servicing southampton" 14.2 to 24.9, "boiler services southampton" 16.8 to 29.9.

Mostly this is averaging, not the homepage dropping. The homepage still ranks about 8-11 for "boiler repair southampton". From the week of 19 Oct Google also started testing services.html (pos 36-50), the new emergency page (24-60) and boiler-repair.html (42-82) for the same searches, and those low positions drag the average down. Three pages competing for one search means none of them breaks through.

The one real slip: "boiler services southampton", where the homepage went from about 11 to 20-32 in the last two weeks.
